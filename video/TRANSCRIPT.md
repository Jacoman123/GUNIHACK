**0:08 · The usual way**

Most drone swarms run on central command. The base tells each drone where to search, by radio. When the link drops, drones finish their job, and wait. The search stalls.

**0:21 · Every drone carries its own notebook**

Vanta has no boss. Each drone carries its own copy of the mission: what's searched, who's where, and where survivors are. Drones in range swap notes, and pass them on.

**0:33 · Leases, not orders**

Drones claim sectors with short leases, renewed while they work. If a drone crashes, or goes quiet, its lease runs out, and a free drone finishes the job.

**0:45 · Back in contact**
